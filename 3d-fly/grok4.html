Welcome to 3D Flying Battle!

This is a simple 3D flying battle game. Your goal is to survive as long as possible, destroy enemies, collect power-ups, and advance through levels.

Controls:

  • W/A/S/D: Move forward/left/back/right
  • Arrow Up/Down/Left/Right: Pitch up/down, roll left/right
  • Space: Shoot bullets
  • Shift: Boost speed (uses energy, recharges over time)

Gameplay Elements:

  • Enemies (Red Spheres): They chase you and shoot. Destroy them for points.
  • Power-Ups (Green Cubes): Collect to restore health or gain shields.
  • Asteroids (Gray Rocks): Avoid or destroy them; collision damages you.
  • Levels: Every 1000 points, level up: more enemies, faster speed.
  • Health: Drops to 0 = Game Over. Regenerates slowly if not damaged.
  • Score Multipliers: Chain kills for bonuses (e.g., 3 in a row = x2 score).
  • Boss Fights: Every 5 levels, a big enemy appears with more health.

Tips:

  • Start slow: Practice flying and shooting in early levels.
  • Use boost wisely: Great for escaping, but don't overuse.
  • Aim ahead of moving enemies.
  • Collect power-ups quickly before they disappear.
